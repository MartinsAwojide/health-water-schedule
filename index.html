🧠 Core Principles (Read First)

  • External cues replace internal signals: You can't rely on "feeling thirsty" or "feeling the urge". Your interoception requires external support.
  • Anchor to actions you already do: Tie new habits to existing routines (e.g., wake up = drink water).
  • Visual > mental: Keep your water bottle in sight at all times. Use timers and check boxes to offload mental tracking.
  • Short toilet time (≤5 min): No phone = no prolonged pressure. This is a non-negotiable rule.
  • Consistency is key: Managing soft consistencies ensures no bleeding or aggravation. Everything flows from this.

🚨 Medical Escalation Triggers

  • Heavy bleeding: Large clots, bleeding that doesn't stop, or blood filling the bowl.
  • Severe pain: Pain that actively prevents sitting or moving normally.
  • Signs of thrombosis: Sudden firm, very painful lump at the site.
  • Fever + bleeding: Could indicate an active infection.
  • Dizziness, weakness, pale skin: Possible anemia from chronic blood loss.
  • No improvement after 6-8 weeks: Despite following this plan consistently.
