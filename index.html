🎯 Your ADHD-Friendly Hemorrhoid Management Schedule

Based on confirmed hemorrhoids | Designed for ADHD + interoception challenges

🧠 Core Principles (Read First)

  • External cues replace internal signals: You can't rely on "feeling thirsty" or "feeling the urge"
  • Anchor to actions you already do: Tie new habits to existing routines
  • Visual > mental: Keep water bottle in sight, use timers, check boxes
  • Short toilet time (≤5 min): No phone = no prolonged hemorrhoid pressure
  • Soft stools = no bleeding: Everything flows from this

Synthetic Support: Docusate 100-200mg or PEG 3350 (Only if natural whole foods are insufficient).

🚨 Stop Everything and See Doctor If:

  • Heavy bleeding: Large clots, bleeding that doesn't stop, blood filling toilet bowl
  • Severe pain: Pain that prevents sitting or moving normally
  • Signs of thrombosed hemorrhoid: Sudden firm, very painful lump at anus
  • Fever + rectal bleeding: Could indicate infection
  • Dizziness/weakness: Possible anemia from chronic blood loss
