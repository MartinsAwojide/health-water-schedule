🎯 Your ADHD-Friendly Hemorrhoid Management Schedule

Based on confirmed hemorrhoids | Designed for ADHD + interoception challenges

🧠 Core Principles (Read First)

  • External cues replace internal signals: You can't rely on "feeling thirsty" or "feeling the urge"
  • Anchor to actions you already do: Tie new habits to existing routines
  • Visual > mental: Keep water bottle in sight, use timers, check boxes
  • Short toilet time (≤5 min): No phone = no prolonged hemorrhoid pressure
  • Soft stools = no bleeding: Everything flows from this
🌅 Morning Routine 6:00 - 8:00 AM
1. Wake + Hydrate (before anything else)
Drink 1 full glass (250-300ml) of water immediately. Keep glass by bed the night before.
2. Take morning medications/supplements
If prescribed: stool softener (docusate) or PEG. Plus any other meds.
3. High-fiber breakfast
Choose: Oats + fruit | Whole grain cereal + berries | Whole wheat toast + avocado
→ Aim for 8-10g fiber
4. Drink another glass of water with breakfast
That's 500-600ml total before you start work.
5. "Bathroom opportunity" 15-20 min after breakfast
Sit for max 3-5 minutes. NO PHONE. If nothing comes, get up. No straining ever.
💡 ADHD Hack: Set your water bottle/glass on top of your phone in the morning so you HAVE to pick it up and drink.
☕ Mid-Morning 8:00 - 12:00 PM
1. Fill your 750ml-1L water bottle
Keep it visible at your workstation. Goal: finish it by lunch.
2. Every 60-90 min: Take 5 sips
Use timer/app/smartwatch if helpful. Or tie to task transitions.
3. Before starting deep work: quick bathroom check
Even if you "don't feel it," sit for 2 min. Prevents missing signals during hyperfocus.
💡 Visual Cue: Put a sticky note on your monitor: "Sip water every time you see this"
🍽️ Lunch 12:00 - 1:00 PM
1. Drink 1 full glass BEFORE eating
Non-negotiable anchor: water before food.
2. Include fiber: legumes/beans OR vegetables OR fruit
Examples: Lentil stew, bean salad, veggie-packed meal, side of fruit.
3. Another glass during/after meal
That's 2 glasses at lunch = ~500ml.
🌤️ Afternoon 1:00 - 6:00 PM
1. Refill water bottle #2
Another 750ml-1L to sip through afternoon.
2. Movement break: 15-20 min walk
Helps gut motility. Can be after lunch or mid-afternoon.
3. Snack with fiber if hungry
Apple, pear, nuts, whole grain crackers + hummus.
4. Bathroom check before leaving work/transitioning activities
Another preventive "opportunity" to avoid missing signals.
🌆 Evening 6:00 - 10:00 PM
1. Dinner: include vegetables/legumes
At least 1 serving of cooked veg or beans. Frozen/canned is fine.
2. Water with dinner
1 glass minimum.
3. Evening sitz bath (if you had bleeding today)
10-15 min in warm water. Can pair with post-shower routine to anchor it.
4. Prep for tomorrow morning
Fill water glass and put it by bed. Lay out morning meds/supplements.
💡 Phone Rule: If you use the bathroom in evening, leave phone outside. Use a visible timer (kitchen timer, watch) for 5 min max.

💊 Daily Supplements/Medications (discuss with your doctor)

Stool Softener: Docusate 100-200mg daily (prevents hard stools)
Fiber Supplement: Psyllium husk or PEG 3350 if diet fiber isn't enough
Hemorrhoid Cream: Apply after BM if itching/burning present
Zinc: 15-30mg daily (wound healing support)
Vitamin C: 500mg daily (collagen synthesis, tissue repair)

🚨 Stop Everything and See Doctor If:

  • Heavy bleeding: Large clots, bleeding that doesn't stop, blood filling toilet bowl
  • Severe pain: Pain that prevents sitting or moving normally
  • Signs of thrombosed hemorrhoid: Sudden firm, very painful lump at anus
  • Fever + rectal bleeding: Could indicate infection
  • Dizziness, weakness, pale skin: Possible anemia from chronic blood loss
  • No improvement after 6-8 weeks: Despite following this plan consistently
